## Time frame

Detection: Sun 11 Oct 2026 16:40 (UTC+3). Event day: Mon 12 Oct 2026 (Japan national day 09:00–14:00, official delegation 11:00–13:00).
Window: 11–13 Oct 2026. Dates are simulated.

## How to read the columns (decisions worth knowing)

- `hours_worked_this_week`: planned total for the whole week, **including** the assignments in the 3-day window.
  Minimum 8, maximum 48; 8 employees sit at 44–48.
- `hours_after` (engine): hours + 8 if the person is NOT already on that shift; unchanged if they are only being moved.
- `current_site_code`: the person's site on 11 Oct; empty if off that day. `current_zone` then is a random home zone.
- `loan_scope_zones`: semicolon-separated zones; only for `category=loan`.
- Certificates are checked against the **shift date**, never today.
- Ranking order (stated in the proposal): requirements met, then travel minutes, then remaining hours, then surplus at the current site.
